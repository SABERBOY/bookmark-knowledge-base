Heartseeker Rogue Endgame Build Guide for Diablo IV Season 15 - Hell's Legacy
Introduction
Heartseeker is a very popular ranged bow build, known for its ease of use and overall simplicity and effectiveness. This is a Cold basic attack build designed for quick and fast gameplay that does well in both single target applications and density, thanks to many different massive multiplicative offerings to buff Heartseeker itself. You no longer need Shard of Verathiel to start this build, making the barrier of entry easier than before! While not very flashy in the visual department, its a go to build for simple, straight forward gameplay and gearing that can effectively do everything well with no inherent weaknesses. 
As recent as season 14, the build has evolved to a spin 2 win state, also known as GoD DH from Diablo 3, making this build considerably more fun, more efficient, and reminiscent of the good ol days of the DH GoD6 Hungering Arrow build. This guide walks you through everything you need to destroy in sanctuary!

Here you learn how to optimize your Heartseeker Rogue Endgame with the correct Legendary and Unique items, Talisman and Charm options, Item Stat Priorities, Runes, and Class Mechanic choices.
Equipment
- We start with Physical Heartseeker as uniques such as Etna's Lost Dagger can take a little bit to get.
- Insight is extremely good item early on. This will carry you well into midgame.
- Ferocious Aspect grants a lot of attack speed, and also eventually a huge damage buff when you get Scoundrel's Kiss. The attack speed alone is what makes this setup at the start feel good to play. Aspect of Rallying Reversal makes sure to keep our ferocity stacks topped off.
- We get vulnerable from Aspect of Accursed Touch.
- At this stage, you want to fill out as many generic and marksman related aspects as possible, while being on the lookout for the Narrows Eye set charms so we can start transitioning out of the need for maxing out Dance of Knives to help deal damage, to other things mentioned later in the other variants.
- We keep Dark Shroud on our bar for now until we get our 5pc charm set.
- Shadow Clone and Preparation are useful early on, but are dropped past the starter variant as they do not scale well with this build.
- Ner rune allows us to gain crit chance from Concealment skill without having to have it on our bar.

Stats

Equipment
- Scoundrel's Kiss gets a ton of value with our Ferocity stacks, so a lot of synergy has been built into this build to take advantage of it.
- We want to roll chance to deal fire/lightning/shadow or poison (anything but cold or physical) damage temper on a 1H weapon to proc Etna's Lost Dagger bonus.
- We also now generate Dark Shroud stacks with the 5pc set automatically, so we drop the skill from our bar.
- Tibault's Will is used to cover the additional resource drain from our Greater Splinter of Anguish.
- Shard of Verathiel makes Heartseeker spend energy, so its important to have the resource temper on a ring to sustain.
- Gladiator's Triumph also provides a major boost, working in tandem with Paingorger's Gauntlets. If you find a better Paingorger's Gauntlets as an item, use that and charm Gladiator's Triumph.
- If you have enough damage and want more speed for speedfarming, swap out Earthstriker's Aspect for Agile Aspect on boots, as it goes over the movement speed cap.
- If you craft an Enigma before reaching Endgame Variant, wear it.

Equipment
- Cold builds are notorious for being bad against Bosses, specifically against unstoppable targets as a lot of our damage comes from crowd control multipliers. This does NOT mean you can't boss effectively. With one swap, a backup amulet with Aspect of the Expectant your boss kill speed increases significantly, which is great for lair boss farming or fighting Mephisto. This is because Aspect of the Expectant is a generic multiplier that does not require the boss to be stunned, whereas Aspect of Frozen Memories only works when the boss is staggered, which takes a while. You can apply this to any of the variants listed.
- We also remove Abyssal Splinter of the Black Soulstone as it doesn't do anything versus bossing if lair bossing for items is the plan.
And that's it! All you have to do to boss effectively.

Charm Bug Explanation:
- There is a bug in the game that allows you to have a 5-p bonus AND 2 Unique Charms active simultaneously. It is a snapshot that you must do at the beginning of every game session and is only required for pushing the highest Pit/Tower tier possible. This bug has been "green-lit" by the developers for this season.
- To successfully enable the bug you need a Seal of the Golden Epiphany + Seal of the Diamond Mind + Hellfire Torch (Of Phoba of the Narrow Eye), 3x Phoba of the Narrow Eye set charms and 2 Unique Charms that benefit the build.
- Equip Seal of the Golden Epiphany with your Hellfire Torch in the bottom slot, and 3 other Phoba of the Sightless Phoba of the Narrow Eye charms to the left and top positions as seen in the Charm diagram, then place Paingorger's Gauntlets and Etna's Lost Dagger in the bottom left and top right positions.
  - Swap you Seal of the Diamond Mind for Seal of the Golden Epiphany and check your two Uniques charms. Both Aspects should be colored in, instead of greyed out as if you've deactivated them.
- Normally we would not educate users on how to abuse a bug like this, but the official stance from the Diablo team is that this is being left in for the season. So it's no longer considered an exploit.
