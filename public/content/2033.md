What are PBR textures?
PBR (Physically Based Rendering) textures are files that enable 3D graphics to be rendered with photorealism. These files have information embedded in them to let rendering engines understand how to reflect light in addition to showing a graphical pattern.
What applications can I use these textures in?
These textures are usable in all common 3D software such as 3ds Max, Blender, Maya, Houdini, Roblox Studio, or game engines like Unity, Unreal Engine, and many more.
