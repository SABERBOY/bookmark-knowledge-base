I’ve been thinking a lot about fitness lately. I am de termined to get into a shape that remains consistent.

So, I often take up fitness challenges to stay healthier and fitter.

I had challenged myself in the past [to get rid of my belly fat in 3 months](https://medium.com/in-fitness-and-in-health/how-to-lose-belly-fat-7a8c46adfda0). I also succeeded in my [“No Sugar Challenge,” which lasted for over 240 days](https://medium.com/in-fitness-and-in-health/heres-what-happened-when-i-didn-t-eat-sugar-for-240-days-998a544be940). But this time, I was motivated to do something more strenuous.

“What’s better than taking up the 2-minute 30-day plank challenge!” I thought to myself. Plank challenges are a popular trend on social media as they can be done anywhere and at any time of the day.

I had seen people share their experiences about how doing planks regularly helped them build six-pack abs. I wanted to check if it was true. More importantly, I wanted to see if I could push my body against its weight!

I’ll be honest — I wanted to fail at this challenge. Not because I didn’t like planking, but because I really didn’t think it was possible to plank for 2 minutes every day for 30 days in a row.
