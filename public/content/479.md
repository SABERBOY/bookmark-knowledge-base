## Background

I am expecting most people who know about Conda and Unity are thinking “Why integrate them?”. I am coming to that. First a little bit of background.

[Conda](https://docs.conda.io/en/latest/)is an open-source, multi-platform and multi-language package management system. It started as part of the [Anaconda](https://www.anaconda.com/) data science environment targetted at Python and R but has proven so useful that it has been spun out on its own.

The primary value of Conda is:

*   It is multi-platform, running on Windows, Mac and Linux,
*   It is multi-language, supporting Python, R, Ruby, Lua, Scala, Java, JavaScript, C/ C++, FORTRAN, and more, and
*   It has a very efficient and advanced [mechanism for **managing dependencies** across platforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conda_(package_manager)).
