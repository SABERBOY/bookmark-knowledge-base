Frozen Orb Sorcerer Endgame Build Guide for Diablo IV Season 15 - Hell's Legacy
Introduction
Frozen Orb faces mechanical issues due to being destroyed by terrain, investing in the damage over time or Ice Spikes produces the best result. This season has a powerful seasonal gem for damage over time that allows it to come out on top but both variants are viable and close in power. Berú of the Cauldron will make your Pyromancy Skills free to cast once you fully stack your Heat, your Mana Costs may be high while stacking.

Equipment
Here you learn how to optimize your Frozen Orb Sorc with the correct Legendary and Unique items, Talisman and Charm options, Item Stat Priorities, Runes, and Class Mechanic choices.
Soul Splinters
Soul Splinters are special gems for this Season. Prioritize equipping them in your Jewelry in the order below.
- Splinter of Hellfire
- Splinter of the Black Soulstone
- Splinter of the Mother
Gear
Aspect of Elemental Acuity or Vulpine's Aspect provides Mana Regeneration if you find it before Tibault's Will.
Aspect of Shredding Blades provides Vulnerable and is important to find.
Runes
CemQua provides a boost to your movement speed for quality of life while navigating the game. You must evade once every 7 seconds to maintain the speed.
CirQue activates Earthen Bulwark to maintain a defensive Barrier.
Charms
Berú of the Cauldron provides stacking damage in the form of Heat with every Pyromancy Skill that is cast.
Drognan's Anguish is a large damage boost if found early.

Gear - Mythic Craft Priority Order
Work through this list as you find the recommended Mythics for the build. Note: You can only equip one Mythic that you craft in the Horadric Cube using the "Upgrade to Mythic" recipe.
- Leoric's Crown provides damage and defense, making it a good choice to craft.
- Raiment of the Infinite provides Damage Reduction, it requires you to use any version of your Teleport every 8 seconds.
- Drognan's Anguish has the largest damage multiplier for this build.
- Onyx Soul allows returns Frozen Orb to you with increased damage as long as terrain doesn't destroy it.
- Orsivane gives additional Enchantments along with damage for keeping Defensive Skills off your Action Bar.
- Tibault's Will provides your Mana Regeneration, it requires you to use Ice Armor or Jah every 5 seconds.
Ring of Starless Skies reduces Mana costs to make stacking your Heat less painful. This can be replaced if you don't mind focusing on stacking with other skills when entering new areas.
Runes
IgniJah gives an extra Teleport every few seconds after casting a Non-Basic Skill. This also activates Tibault's Will.
NeoMot provides additional defense.
Lac and Que are other options for defense. Ohm or Vex can provide additional damage.
Set Charms
Berú of the Cauldron provides stacking damage in the form of Heat with every Pyromancy Skill that is cast. At max stacks your Pyromancy Skills become free to cast with additional damage and a chance for area damage.
- Pyromancy Skills is the priority for this build, Core Skills is another option.
- Single Resistances are great defense, some of the best include Fire, Lightning, and Physical but anything is acceptable and a variety can help against all content.
- Stone of Jordan is a defensive option that would desire Fire Resistance on all Charms instead of any other Resistance. This would also mean getting Fire Resistance on gear instead of All Resistance.
Unique Charms
Fists of Fate as a mythic is the largest multiplier.
Tal Rasha's Iridescent Loop provides additional damage and is easily activated by Familiar: Prismatic Familiar.
Rustbitten Dirk is your best single target option for defeating Lair Bosses.
Stone of Jordan provides offense and defense when invested into.
Godslayer Crown provides quality of life by bringing the enemies to you while increasing your damage.
Endurant Faith or Soulbrand can provide defense to help stay alive.

Skills
Learn more about the Skill Tree and Gameplay Rotation for the Frozen Orb Sorc here.
Skills
Frozen Orb is your primary damage, this build scales the burning damage.
Arc Lash provides Ferocity, movement speed, and Mana.
Hydra provides cooldown reduction and healing. Keep one active.
Teleport provides Ferocity and movement speed. It can be reset with Overpower from Inferno.
Ice Armor provides Barrier, Armor, and Unstoppable.
Inferno provides Overpower that can be used to reset Teleport.
Skill Rotation
Cast Inferno when available to stack Overpower for Teleport: Blaze.
Use Teleport for mobility and to stack Heat, it also provides Ferocity when hitting enemies.
Cast Frozen Orb at enemies, avoid terrain that will destroy it before it returns.
Use Arc Lash as needed for Mana and can maintain the movement speed every 5 seconds.
Maintain Ice Armor for Barrier and additional defense from Ice Armor: Permafrost.
Maintain one Hydra for additional cooldown reduction.
Enchantments
Frozen Orb has a chance to trigger additional Orbs with any Skill Cast, providing additional damage.
Teleport provides mobility for quality of life. Once you reach Enchantment Master then you will want different elements for the damage bonus.

Paragon
Progress your Frozen Orb Sorc through the Pit to rank up your Glyphs. Carefully check your Paragon Boards to ensure correct order and rotation.
Board Rush
The Board Rush setup prioritizes Legendary Nodes as quickly as possible for the fastest boost in damage with the least number of points. From here you can expand into nearby Glyphs until you are ready to transition to the next setup. Choosing this board setup requires changing boards later to optimize.
Glyphs
Flamefeeder is always active since this build focuses on Burning damage.
Elementalist activates after dealing damage with different elements, all should easily activate.
Tactician activates after using a Defensive Skill, this will easily activate with Teleport and Ice Armor.
Pyromaniac stacks damage as you cast Pyromancy Skills.
Eliminator provides additional damage against Elites, this includes Bosses.
You can level them to each Legendary Bonus in the listed order as a priority but generally you can level all Glyphs evenly after reaching the Legendary Bonus on each one.
