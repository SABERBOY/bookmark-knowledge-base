Assignments and Projects

The bulk of your assignments will entail implementing a full-blown 3D package called Scotty3D, which encompasses modeling, rendering, and animation. You can find the skeleton code [here](https://github.com/CMU-Graphics/Scotty3D), along with a showcase of some results from previous years. Also make sure to check out the [Scotty3D documentation](https://cmu-graphics.github.io/Scotty3D-docs/).
