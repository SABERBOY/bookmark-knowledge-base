What's new
Introducing: Needle Mesh Baker
Turn heavy 3D models into production-ready assets, in your browser. 880,000 triangles down to 6,000 in seconds — geometry reduced, the original's detail baked into PBR textures, and nothing ever uploaded. Ideal for sculpts, scans, CAD and AI-generated models.
OpenUSD and MaterialX, in the BrowserNEW
Strongly improved OpenUSD 26.05 and MaterialX 1.39.5 support across Needle USD Viewer, Needle Cloud, and Needle Engine — host and share USD and MaterialX, with seamless three.js integration, lights, shadows and displacement, and a huge MaterialX performance boost.
Needle Engine for Blender 1.0.0NEW
A milestone release: completely redesigned UI, reflection probes, improved lightmapping, supercharged EventLists, 15+ new components, and support for Blender 4.2 LTS through 5.1.
MaterialX: Displacement & ShadowsNEW
MaterialX materials now support vertex displacement (procedural and texture-based), realtime shadows, displacement animation, and alpha mode detection — all built into Needle Engine 5.
