Xaero’s Minimap Mod (26.3, 1.20.1) – Never Lose Your Way Again
Xaero’s Minimap Mod displays nearby terrain, players, mobs, and dropped items right in the corner of your screen. This mod matches the classic vanilla Minecraft style and runs smoothly during gameplay. You get a clear view both above ground and deep inside caves thanks to its automatic cave mode. It also lets you mark important spots with custom waypoints and creates an automatic deathpoint whenever you die. Two editions exist to fit different servers, offering a full version with every feature and a fair-play version without cave maps or radar for balanced PvP.
Features:
- Custom Shape and Position: Choose between a rotating square or circle minimap, pick from multiple sizes, and move it anywhere on your screen down to a single pixel.
- Automatic Cave Mode: Switches to underground mapping when you enter caves or buildings based on the solid roof size above your head.
- Waypoints and Deathpoints: Save unlimited locations that appear on the map and in the world, share them in chat, teleport to them if allowed, and find your gear easily with automatic skull markers on death.
- Chunk and Light Overlays: Turn on a chunk grid, highlight green slime chunks, or set a custom light overlay to spot dark blocks where hostile mobs can spawn.
- Info Under the Map: Displays your coordinates by default, with extra toggles for current biome name, light level, game time, and camera angles.
- Keeps the look of vanilla Minecraft with two color modes: Vanilla map colors or Accurate block and biome textures.
- Lets you lock north so the map stays still and shows your character direction with an arrow instead.
- Organizes saved markers into waypoint sets and separates local waypoints from long-distance global waypoints.
- Adjusts map shading with Terrain Depth and Terrain Slopes options.
- Works smoothly alongside Xaero’s World Map by sharing generated map textures for better performance, and shows claimed chunks from Open Parties and Claims.
- Includes privacy options to hide waypoint coordinates and server addresses during live streams or videos.
- Supports multiple languages and real-time server settings for multiplayer worlds.
Entity Radar Indicators:
- Mobs appear as yellow dots or mob head icons, with separate color options for friendly and hostile mobs.
- Players show up on the radar as white dots or player heads, with an option to display names.
- Dropped items appear as red dots so you can spot loot nearby.
- Other objects like arrows and item frames show up as purple dots and can be toggled off in settings.
How to Use:
- Press Y in-game to open the main settings menu and customize the map size, shape, position, radar, and extra info lines.
- Press B anytime to create a new waypoint at your current spot.
- Press U to open the waypoints list, where you can edit markers, sort them into sets, share them in chat, or click Teleport if you have permission.
- Press Z to temporarily enlarge the minimap for a wider view of the area around you.
- Press I to zoom in and O to zoom out on the minimap.
- Open the standard Minecraft Controls menu if you want to rebind any of these default keys.
Configuration:
- minimapItemId:minecraft:compass: Add this line in the config file to hide the minimap unless you hold or carry a compass in your hotbar.
- xaero.minimap.enforced_server_profile: Permission node used on servers to enforce minimap rules based on player ranks.
- xaerominimap:no_minimap: Potion effect ID that disables the minimap for affected players.
- xaerominimap:no_entity_radar: Potion effect ID that turns off the entity radar (append _harmful to make any of these effects count as harmful).
- xaerominimap:no_waypoints: Potion effect ID that disables player waypoints.
- xaerominimap:no_cave_maps: Potion effect ID that turns off underground cave mapping.
