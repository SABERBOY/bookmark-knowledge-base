## post by sacb0y on Jul 29, 2024

[![Image 23](https://dub1.discourse-cdn.com/unity/user_avatar/discussions.unity.com/sacb0y/48/101976_2.png)](https://discussions.unity.com/u/sacb0y)

[sacb0y](https://discussions.unity.com/u/sacb0y)

[Jul 2024](https://discussions.unity.com/t/why-is-lit-shader-so-big/914011/6 "Post date")

Depending on how you make a shader, every setting change or toggle can create a variant. internally there is not one shader but many based on need, these are variants. The one shader is built many times with different configurations if materials, graphics settings or other stuff needs it.

Similar materials don’t need an additional variant.

Less varied graphics settings need less variants. etc.

With forward+ less maximum lights affects variants too.
