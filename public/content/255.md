Jacco Bikker<p>What is the correct FOV to use in a game?</p><p>Think of your monitor as a window. Lines from your eye to the monitor (in the real world) should continue in the same direction in the virtual world.</p><p>You get this by taking the angle between the two solid lines: This is the correct FOV.</p><p>The default field of view in Starfield is 85. This may be correct, depending on how large your monitor is, and how close you are to it.</p><p>Helldivers 2 uses a vertical FOV of 55: roughly 85 vertically on a 16:9 monitor.</p>
