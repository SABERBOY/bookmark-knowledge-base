### Condottiere-Clarice Starling

Clarice Starling is a young elven sentinel, resolute and perceptive. Chosen by her elders, she ventures into the human world to investigate the Pope’s scheme surrounding the Holy Stone. Loyal to nature and ancient magic, she allies with the exiled knight Magus and ultimately guides the Holy Stone back to elven protection.
