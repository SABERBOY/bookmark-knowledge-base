# GET YOUR ANIMATION DEGREE ONLINE

## CREATE YOUR FUTURE

From dazzling visual effects to stunning computer graphics, learn the skills needed to bring your digital art to life for film, TV, games and more.

#### Classes start soon, so enroll now and begin your journey.

## LEARN.

### Start Your Path

Each step of your journey brings new knowledge and proficiency. Begin with a foundation in art then explore the entire production pipeline as you learn about the computer animation process from start to finish, then choose an area of focus in:

**Environment and Character Design:**Learn how to integrate your creations into games, movies and more.

**Visual Effects:**Learn how to create visual effects and incorporate them to live action, from green screen methodology to motion tracking and more.

**Character Animation:**Create impressive character motions and build epic battle scenes from the ground up.

## Create.

### Grab Your Gear

Animators rely on high-performing gear and software to bring their creations to life. As an animation student, the TechKit® becomes your fully-armed battle station to design out-of-this-world VFX and animations for film, TV and video games.  
 Our industry-experienced instructors curated a selection of Animation textbooks to help you get inspired to create your future as an animator in entertainment.*  

[Video 2](https://vimeo.com/280975330)

## Animate Your Future

Our alumni are in the industry doing amazing things! Whether compositing for blockbusters or working on projects for companies like Disney or brands like Marvel, our grads are building their place in the industry. From Oscar® nominations to VMA® wins, everyone’s path to success is different. What will yours be?
