## Welcome

Take a look at Monster Survivors Documentation

Monster Survivors is a complete 2D horde survival game, inspired by Vampire Survivors and Survivor.io. Suitable for developers of most skill levels, from beginner to pro. Includes everything you need to start building your own game of this genre: player movement, abilities, enemy behaviors, boss fight mechanics, stage creator, and so much more - all with original art.
