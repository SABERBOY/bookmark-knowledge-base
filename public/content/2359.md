Just one month after Pixel Flow became a mega hit, a new game appeared. Color Blaze Shooter by Vietnamese Studio Amobear 🇻🇳
The problem?
It was a 1-to-1 copy of Pixel Flow.
Same mechanics.
Same visual style.
Same core loop.
On March 1, the game was removed from both Google Play and the iOS App Store.
Copycats in mobile gaming are nothing new.
But this one did not last long. Even in its short lifetime, it generated $20M! ($10m in IAP & $10m in Ad Revenue)
Was this a master legal move by Scopely after the acquisition of Loom Games, or is this a signal from the stores that they will be more stringent on copycats moving forward?
