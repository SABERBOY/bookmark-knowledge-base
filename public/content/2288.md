FAQ
What is Animation Throwdown?
Animation Throwdown is a collectible card game (CCG) featuring popular animated TV show characters, developed by Kongregate and Synapse Games.
How do you play Animation Throwdown?
In Animation Throwdown, you build a deck of cards based on characters, items, and combos from shows like Family Guy, Futurama, American Dad, Bob's Burgers, and King of the Hill, then battle AI or other players in strategic card matches.
What are the main progression systems in Animation Throwdown?
Players progress by collecting and upgrading cards, leveling up heroes, and unlocking new battle arenas as they advance through single-player campaigns and player-versus-player (PvP) modes.
Does Animation Throwdown have multiplayer or PvP features?
Yes, Animation Throwdown offers player-versus-player battles, guild-based events, and leaderboards where you can compete with other players.
On which platforms can you play Animation Throwdown?
Animation Throwdown is available to play on web browsers through Kongregate and is also offered as a mobile app on iOS and Android devices.
