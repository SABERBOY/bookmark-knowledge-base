Biomes O’ Plenty Mod (26.3, 1.20.1) – Explore Lush New Terrain
Biomes O’ Plenty Mod adds over 50 unique biomes to the Overworld, Nether, and End. Each new area fits the vanilla Minecraft style and gives you fresh landscapes to explore. Along with new terrain, this mod fills your world with custom trees, plants, flowers, and building blocks.
Gem Distribution:
Features:
- New Biomes Across Dimensions: Generates over 50 unique vanilla-style biomes across the Overworld, Nether, and End.
- Custom Plants and Blocks: Fills the new areas with fresh trees, flowers, plants, and building blocks.
- Brings diverse Overworld landscapes such as Alps, Cherry Blossom Grove, Lavender Fields, Mystic Grove, Redwood Forest, and Oasis.
- Expands the Nether with dark and eerie areas like Fungi Forest, Corrupted Sands, Undergarden, and Visceral Heap.
- Lets you shrink biome sizes with the Small Biomes setting, which works as the opposite of the Large Biomes world type.
- Gives you a No Parasite option that stops the Parasitic Heap biome from spawning in your world.
- Allows you to generate only realistic biomes or only fantasy biomes to match how you want to play.
- Includes a Missing Biomes mode that mixes vanilla areas with classic biomes from older Minecraft versions, including Rainforest, Savanna, Seasonal Forest, Shrubland, Tundra, Boreal Forest, and Lush Swamp.
- Changes biomes to match the current real-life season when you turn on the Seasonal option.
- Updates biomes for United States holiday seasons like Halloween and Christmas through the Holiday option.
