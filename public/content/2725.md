Changes to Vanilla Spells
Along with the new spells listed above, Apotheosis modifies a number of existing vanilla spells as follows:
- Ball Lightning, Omega disk, plasma, Pinpoint of Light & music note spells only hit every 15 frames instead of every 1 frame
- Summon Fish hits every 10 frames instead of every 1 frame
- Some spells have had their mana cost drastically reduced, which includes but is not limited to: Death Cross, Chaotic Transmutation, Intense Concentrated Light, Tier 2&3 Spitter Bolts, and various other spells
- Bubbly Bounce no longer does self damage
- Arrow no longer has increased recoil
- Giant Explosion on Drunk & Slimy Enemies now has unlimited charges
- Spells to Power can only spawn in Heaven, Hell & other high-tier spell locations
- Path of Dark Flame can appear in lower tier spell pools
- Matter Eater can now dig through cursed rock
- Giga Death Cross now does much, much more damage and uses green wand particles to match the spell's colour
- Summon Egg can summon Apotheosis eggs
- Unstable Crystal can now be affected by Larpa spells
- Chainsaw now costs 12 mana
- Piercing Shot is reworked into Penetrating Shot.
- Chain Bolt can now repeatedly hit a single target
Miscellaneous Vanilla Changes
- The Blinded status only lasts for 50% of it's regular duration.
- Most elemental stones grant their beneficial effects just by being in the player's inventory.

Changes to Vanilla Perks
Along with the new perks listed above, Apotheosis modifies a number of existing vanilla perks as follows:
- Low Recoil no longer has the downside of slowing down your projectiles, being reworked into No Recoil
- Contact Damage additionally grants melee immunity
- Faster Movement & Faster Levitation have been removed, as both of their effects are provided by Haste
- Projectile Repulsion Field no longer exists
- Toxic Immunity & Gas Blood now additionally grant immunity to enemy attacks using the Toxic damage type
- Vampirism's healing now scales with the player's maximum health
- Spatial Awareness marks Essence of Fungus with a small dot and Runestone of Alchemy cave with a potion icon
