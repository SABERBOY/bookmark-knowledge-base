Falling Tree Mod (26.3, 1.20.1) – Clear Forests in Seconds
Falling Tree Mod lets you chop down a whole tree by breaking just one log. You do not have to hit the bottom block, because breaking any connected log brings all the wood down. Larger trees take longer to cut, and your axe loses durability for each block so gameplay stays fair. When you only want to remove a single block, hold Shift and chop as usual.
Features:
- Lets you customize which log and leaf blocks count as a tree in the config file.
- Allows you to choose which tools can chop trees down, such as extra axes or any item at all.
- Gives you an option to turn leaf breaking on or off.
- Disables the mod effect while you sneak, with a setting to reverse this control.
- Changes your chopping speed based on the size of the tree.
- Damages tools based on the number of blocks in the tree, or lets you turn tool damage off.
- Keeps tools at 1 durability if chopping a tree would otherwise break them.
Tree Cutting Modes:
- Instantaneous mode makes the whole tree fall right away when you break one log.
- Shift Down mode slowly moves upper logs down as you cut, so you do not have to climb up to reach top blocks.
- Fall Items mode makes the whole tree fall with an animation when you break a single log.
- Fall Blocks mode makes the whole tree fall onto the floor with an animation after you break one log.
