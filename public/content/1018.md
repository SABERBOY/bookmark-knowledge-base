DPlayer is a lovely HTML5 danmaku video player to help people build video and danmaku easily.
DPlayer supports:
- Streaming formats
  - HLS
  - FLV
  - MPEG DASH
  - WebTorrent
  - Any other custom streaming formats
- Media formats
  - MP4 H.264
  - WebM
  - Ogg Theora Vorbis
- Features
  - Danmaku
  - Screenshot
  - Hotkeys
  - Quality switching
  - Thumbnails
  - Subtitle
