Large Ore Deposits Mod (26.3, 1.20.1) – Track Rich Buried Veins
Large Ore Deposits Mod adds rare, supermassive ore veins hidden deep underground across your world. Finding one of these giant deposits turns regular mining into an exciting treasure hunt and supplies your base with huge amounts of resources. You can explore the surface and look for special flower circles that point to rich veins buried below. This mod works out of the box with both vanilla and modded ores, and gives you simple config files and commands to customize how every deposit spawns.
Features:
- Spawns rare, giant ore veins underground that make long mining trips much more rewarding.
- Includes ready-to-use configs for common vanilla and modded ores, including aluminum, amethyst, ancient debris, coal, cobalt, copper, diamond, emerald, gold, iridium, iron, lapis, lead, nether gold, nether quartz, nickel, osmium, palladium, platinum, redstone, rhodium, ruby, ruthenium, sapphire, silver, tin, topaz, uranium, and zinc.
- Lets you build mixed ore deposits with custom proportions of different ores in the same vein.
- Places surface clues above buried deposits, using circles of vanilla flowers, Glowroses, and Mystical flowers by default.
- Allows ore veins to generate directly under specific structures when you set the structure name as the surface indicator ID in the config.
- Supports separate spawn settings and limits for different dimensions and biomes.
- Lets you add support for any custom ore through in-game commands or by placing config files in the .minecraft/config/adlods/Deposits folder.
- Gives you control over standard vanilla ore generation using config files inside the .minecraft/config/adlods/VanillaOres folder.
- Includes built-in commands to test, locate, and tweak ore deposits right inside the game.
Commands:
- /lods reload_configs: reloads all config files right away so changes take effect without restarting the game.
- /lods define_deposit <name> <ore> <rarity> <size> <altitude>: creates a new deposit and its config file using a unique name, ore ID (such as minecraft:diamond_ore), chunk rarity (such as 2400), block size limits (such as 300..500), and Y-coordinate limits (such as -30..60).
- /lods remove_deposit <deposit_name>: unloads the specified deposit and deletes its config file.
- /lods list_active_deposits: shows the names of all loaded and active deposits in chat.
- /lods list_all_generated: writes a list of all generated deposits and their coordinates in the current world to the latest.log file.
- /lods list_generated_around: logs generated deposits and their coordinates for nearby chunks within your line of sight.
- /lods open_configs_folder: opens the config folder for this mod.
- /lods open_latest_log: opens the latest.log file.
- /lods open_deposit_config <deposit_name>: opens the config file for the specified deposit.
- /lods spawn_deposit_here <deposit_name>: spawns an ore deposit at your current position and replaces air blocks for quick testing.
- /lods spawn_deposit_underground <deposit_name>: spawns an ore deposit in your current chunk using the settings from its config file.
- /lods spawn_deposit_at_chunk <deposit_name> <chunkX> <chunkZ>: spawns an ore deposit in the chosen chunk if that chunk is currently loaded.
- /lods spawn_deposit_at_pos <deposit_name> <posX> <posY> <posZ>: spawns an ore deposit at the exact coordinates if replaceable blocks are present there.
- /lods strip_chunk: removes all blocks except ores in the chunk at your current position.
- /lods strip_chunks_around: removes all blocks except ores in the chunks around your current position.
- /lods dress_chunk: restores removed blocks in the chunk at your current position.
- /lods dress_chunks_around: restores removed blocks in the chunks around your current position.
- /lods dress_all_chunks: restores blocks in all chunks that were stripped earlier.
