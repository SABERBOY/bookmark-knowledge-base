Soft Drinks Mod (1.18.2, 1.18.1) – Better Water Bottles
Soft Drinks Mod introduces into the game new types of water bottles for players to consume. While players don’t really need to consume water to survive, many still want Mojang to integrate this feature as it is essential for survival in real life. With Soft Drinks, players can utilize bubbling water and sparkling water in order to make their thirst go away. At the same time, players have 10% to grant a new special buff effect if they consume these types of water bottles constantly. Soft Drinks would definitely be a great addition for the game as players are always longing for some sort of drinks to consume during their expeditions.
