I am currently at ClickHouse Labs and will not be taking new Ph.D. students. We will announce ClickHouse Summer 2027 Ph.D. internships in September 2026.
        
        I am an Associate Professor with Indefinite Tenure of Databaseology in the Computer Science Department at Carnegie Mellon University. My research interest is in database management systems, specifically self-driving / autonomous architectures, transaction processing systems, and large-scale data analytics. At CMU, I am a member of the Database Group and the Parallel Data Laboratory. I was the co-founder and CEO of the OtterTune start-up but then it died. I currently lead research at ClickHouse Labs and it is awesome.
