## Summon Taikasauva

Keep Watching Next video in 8 seconds

![Image 2: Disambig](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/ucp-internal-test-starter-commons/images/6/69/Disambig.png/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/18?cb=20200818143606)_This article is about the spell. For the enemy, see [Taikasauva](https://noita.fandom.com/wiki/Taikasauva "Taikasauva")._

**Summon Taikasauva** summons forth a flying wand to fight for you. The [wand](https://noita.fandom.com/wiki/Wands "Wands") created is randomly generated, just like any other wand you find in the world. It behaves just like a charmed enemy and will retaliate if you shoot at it.

It will drop gold nuggets when defeated, as well as the wand itself for you to pick up.

It has a single charge.

## Spells[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fnoita.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSummon_Taikasauva%3Faction%3Dedit%26section%3D1&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

This spell calls forth Tier 3 wands, which will spawn with a random selection of [Tier 2 spells](https://noita.fandom.com/wiki/Spell_Tiers#Tier_2_Spells "Spell Tiers") most of the time.

Wand generators pull spells from one tier lower, so a tier 3 wand will have tier 2 spells in it for example. There is also a 10% chance that a wand will take a projectile from a higher tier. This check is done multiple times until the 10% roll fails, meaning the chosen projectile can be from many tiers higher. However, considering the chances of a Tier 3 wand spawning with a Tier 4 spell or higher are extremely low, the best you can hope for is a random [Tier 3](https://noita.fandom.com/wiki/Spell_Tiers#Tier_3_Spells "Spell Tiers") Projectile spell.

## Tips[[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fnoita.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSummon_Taikasauva%3Faction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en&metadata=article-registration-edit-article-section "Sign in to edit")]

*   As you cannot control what type of wand this spell calls forth, it can potentially be extremely dangerous to you if, for example, it contains [Earthquake](https://noita.fandom.com/wiki/Earthquake "Earthquake") or [Nuke](https://noita.fandom.com/wiki/Nuke "Nuke") spells.
*   This spell can also be used to reanimate any existing dropped wands if they are close by when you cast it.
*   With the right combination of spells, this spell can be used to generate countless spells and wands.
