Support Page
UMotion has an official support page similar to Unity® Answers. Search for existing answers or ask a new question. This is the official place to get support from the developer and the community. You can post questions as a guest (no registration required!), login via Facebook or create your personal account.
For questions not intended for public consumption, you can use the private Email Support.
