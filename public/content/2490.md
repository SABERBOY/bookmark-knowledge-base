Noir isn't a genre we see much of these days: stories that lean into the classic tropes like voice-over narration, a lone private detective protagonist, moody atmosphere, betrayal. But what I didn't realize was how much of a fan I was of this modern twist on the genre. There's something Zen about listening to John Sugar wax poetic on the nature of things as he drives through the streets of LA in a vintage Stingray convertible. Even with a story revolving around a missing girl, murder, and some pretty dark characters, something about Sugar's ever-calm demeanor makes the entire thing kind of relaxing. It's also a meditation on the beauty of LA and a love letter to '40s and '50s cinema, as elements of what Sugar witnesses harken back to famous noir movies of the past. This story is best enjoyed going in knowing as little as possible about it. It's more than you expect. I promise it won't disappoint. -- Vanessa
