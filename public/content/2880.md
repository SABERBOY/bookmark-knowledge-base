Whether you've always dreamed of becoming a UI/UX designer or you're a developer looking to sharpen your design instincts, this interactive course teaches you to see interfaces the way professionals do. You'll learn the core fundamentals that separate great designs from mediocre ones: white space, contrast, scale, alignment, color theory, typography, and visual hierarchy.

But this isn't passive learning! Throughout the course, you'll test your design-eye with nearly 40 interactive UI challenges, participate in 12 design challenges in Figma, and engage with real-world component design from buttons and cards to navigations, hero sections, testimonials, and full layouts.

With over 16 hours of video content and access to an exclusive Discord community, you'll move from understanding design principles to confidently applying them. Whether you're designing your first prototype or refining an existing portfolio, this course gives you the structured feedback and hands-on practice to level up faster than tutorials or YouTube videos ever could.
