_[Laid-Back Camp](https://www.cbr.com/tag/laid-back-camp/)_ is [an overlooked seinen manga/anime series](https://www.cbr.com/underrated-seinen-anime-2010s/) that's perfect for fans of [the easygoing _iyashikei_ subgenre](https://www.cbr.com/iyashikei-anime-healing-genre-soothe-pandemic-stress/). _Iyashikei_ anime tend to have minimal conflict and drama, and instead soothe the viewer with pleasant episodic plots and charming characters. Many of these titles fit [the "cute girls doing cute things" trend](https://www.cbr.com/laid-back-camp-season-2-episode-2-new-year/) as well, including _Laid-Back Camp_.

Overall, _Laid-Back Camp_ puts more emphasis on its unique premise than any particular plot, with protagonist Nadeshiko and her friends simply wandering from one campsite to another and [learning a lot about camping along the way](https://www.cbr.com/dr-stone-vinland-saga-anime-edutainment-trend/). Anyone looking for some easy anime viewing can try out the _Laid-Back Camp_ anime and manga, both of which are widely available.

## The Plot Of Laid-Back Camp

![Image 10: group photo of characters from Laid-Back Camp](https://static0.cbrimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/Laid-back-camp-1.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

_Laid-Back Camp_ has a straightforward and episodic plot, following protagonist Nadeshiko and her classmates and friends as they explore Japan's countryside, one rustic campsite at a time. This gives Nadeshiko and her pals a minor character arc revolving around their increasing experience and expertise with camping, and [they challenge themselves in new and creative ways in the great outdoors](https://www.cbr.com/laid-back-camp-season-2-episode-6-nadeshiko-solo-camping/). They can also rely on their friend, [the veteran solo camper Shima Rin](https://www.cbr.com/laid-back-camp-season-2-premiere/), who knows a bit of everything about camping.

This is an "edutainment" anime that puts more emphasis on [the joy of learning than the actual plot and characters](https://www.cbr.com/best-wholesome-anime-manga-romances/), although many anime fans will find plenty to like about Nadeshiko and her friends, and they get minor but appreciable character arcs along the way. The characters have no particular end goal in mind -- they simply let their camping hobby lead the way, and there's always something new to explore out there. This gives _Laid-Back Camp_ the potential to continue for quite some time, as Japan has no shortage of fascinating natural features to explore and campsites for Nadeshiko and the others to visit.

## Where To Read The Laid-Back Camp Manga

![Image 11: laid back camp](https://static0.cbrimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/laid-back-camp.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

The _Laid-Back Camp_ manga series is currently ongoing, and fans should note that there are not yet any hardback deluxe editions or omnibus editions of the manga, though the individual paperback volumes are easy enough to find from a variety of retailers. As of this writing, 10 volumes of the _Laid-Back Camp_ manga series are available in English, with volume 11 being slated to release on April 26th, 2022.

Interested manga fans can [visit Barnes & Noble's online catalog](https://www.barnesandnoble.com/s/laid%20back%20camp) and find all available _Laid-Back Camp_ volumes there, and they can also download digital editions of these volumes on the Nook e-reader for a discounted price. Similarly, manga fans can find all these volumes [in Amazon's books department](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=laid-back+camp&i=stripbooks&crid=13FP4WEPOUOEE&sprefix=laid-back+camp%2Cstripbooks%2C85&ref=nb_sb_noss_1&tag=cbr-ai-2641893-20&ascsubtag=UUcbrUeUpU340635&asc_refurl=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.cbr.com%2Flaid-back-camp-where-watch-anime-read-manga%2F&asc_campaign=Short-Term), either paperback print volumes or digital volumes with the Kindle. [Right Stuf Anime likewise stocks the paperback volumes](https://www.rightstufanime.com/category/Manga?keywords=laid-back%20camp) of _Laid-Back Camp_, albeit without an option for digital releases.

## Where To Watch The Laid-Back Camp Anime

![Image 12: laid back camp's three main characters](https://static0.cbrimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/laid-back-camp-characters.jpg?q=50&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=1.5)

Currently, the _Laid-Back Camp_ anime has two short seasons to its name, although Western fans have very limited options for watching it. Notably, there is no Western DVD release of _Laid-Back Camp_, making streaming the main option. [Crunchyroll features every episode of](https://beta.crunchyroll.com/series/GRWEW95KR/laid-back-camp)[_Laid-Back Camp_](https://beta.crunchyroll.com/series/GRWEW95KR/laid-back-camp) and streams these episodes in Japanese dub with a wide variety of subtitles, including English, Latin American Spanish, French, Geman and Arabic, among others.

In addition, the Crunchyroll release of _Laid-Back Camp_ features a bonus live-action season titled _Hanamori Yumiri's Beginner Solo Camping_, which depicts live-action re-enactments of key scenes and camping gear from the original anime.
