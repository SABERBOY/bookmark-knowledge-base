Does looking at all those curves in your Blender scene induce stress and confusion?
Often times importing motion capture or blocking a character animation will generate F-Curve channels that do nothing. They might just have one keyframe or maybe multiple keyframes with no change in value.
You can get rid of the specific F-Curves you want with the Delete Static Channels add-on for Blender.
New in v1.1.0: The Delete Static Keys operator goes beyond removing entire channels - it intelligently removes redundant keyframes that don't contribute to your animation's shape. Perfect for cleaning up dense motion capture data or auto-keyed animations while preserving the essential motion. Use it to optimize your F-Curves without losing animation quality!
Unlike Blender's built-in Decimate command, Delete Static Keys preserves your interpolation modes and only removes truly redundant keyframes, making it ideal for animation blocking and professional workflows.
