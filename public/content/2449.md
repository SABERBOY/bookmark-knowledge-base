Hammer of the Ancients Barbarian Endgame Build Guide for Diablo IV Season 15 - Hell's Legacy
Introduction
Hammer of the Ancients returns in Season 15 as one of the strongest Core skill in Barbarians' arsenal. This generator-less build once again delivers the scriptures of the Bonk God with devastating efficiency.
The The Relentless Heart makes each Hammer of the Ancients apply a stackable debuff to your foes, while the Explosive upgrade massively extends your reach. Later on, using a couple combos such as Ring of Red Furor with Locran's Talisman as well as Mighty Throw: Overpower with Banished Lord's Talisman skyrockets your damage.
If you missed Hammer of the Ancients and prayed for the Apostle of the Bonk God to return, know that your prayers have been answered!

Equipment
Here you learn how to optimize your Hammer of the Ancients Endgame Barbarian with the correct Legendary and Unique items, Talisman and Charm options, Item Stat Priorities, Runes, and Class Mechanic choices.
Key Stats
- Cooldown Reduction: Prioritize 30% to 40% Cooldown reduction.
- %Fury generation: Aim for 50% to 60% from Rallying Cry: Booming
- Fury per second: Aim for a minimum of 40 Fury per second from affixes and Insight.
Equipment
- Leoric's Crown, easy to get and doubles the effects of Gems and Season 15 Splinters
- Stone of Jordan, easy to get and provides decent offense and massive defensive boost.
  - NOTE: Make sure that your Physical resistance value is the highest using charms, affixes and tempers
- Insight, easy to craft as a stat stick with obscene amounts of Fury generation, +%Critical Strike chance and x%Critical Strike Damage
  - Enchant the +Weapon damage into Strength
- Stealth, easy to craft early game alternative to Enigma.
  - Enchant the Impairment reduction into +Maximum Life
- Infinity, craftable with strong offensive value, added defenses and effortless Vulnerable application to close targets
  - Enchant the +%Gold drop rate into Strength
  - The implicit +%Lucky hit chance to deal a Crushing Blow is very strong to quickly make targets Injured (after which it stops activating)
Seasonal itemization
Main splinters
- Abyssal Splinter of Anguish offers valuable stats early on
- Abyssal Splinter of Pain to bolster our defenses
Situational splinters
- (Combat oriented) Abyssal Splinter of the Mother is especially good for the Unhindered status, adds another source of Vulnerable
- (Pushing oriented) Abyssal Splinter of the Black Soulstone offers a stacking damage bonus when pushing
- (Farming oriented) Abyssal Splinter of Lies is extremely valuable when farming
- (Farming oriented) Abyssal Splinter of Skarn is extremely valuable when farming
Runes
- Mal + Sol : Long lasting Damage Reduction buff
- Cir + Gar : Early on critical strike chance bonus
Talisman
- Charm set: This variant uses the 5p Sescheron set for the massively increased Damage and Toughness.
- Charm affixes: +Martial/Core skill ranks > +Maximum Fury | Single resistances > %Armor.
- Seal: Prioritize a legendary Seal with +1 slot
Key Stats
- Cooldown Reduction: Prioritize 50% to 60% Cooldown reduction.
  - This allows you to have Wrath of the Berserker up permanently with a little help from your Shouts and Marshal.
- %Fury generation: Aim for 50% to 72% from Rallying Cry: Booming (bonus becomes marginal above rank 19).
- Fury per second: Aim for a minimum of 45 Fury per second from affixes and Insight, Once multiplied by your resource generation bonus, Ramaladni's Magnum Opus Fury drain becomes manageable.
- Max Fury: Aim for 200 to 300 maximum Fury.
- Resource Cost Reduction: NONE as this would negatively impact the Ring of Red Furor combos.
Equipment (Midgame variant)
- The Relentless Heart as the signature Hammer of the Ancients Unique, debuff the monsters you hit with a stacking damage multiplier.
  - NOTE: For Speedfarm, replace it with Enigma to gain access to the Sorcerer's Teleport (make sure to enchant the +%Movement Speed into Max resource).
- Ring of Red Furor removes the need for Critical Strike chance if you spend 30 Fury before each cast and adds an extra damage bonus. Since spending more Fury increases this bonus additively, it's best to only spend the minimum required.
  - When paired with Locran's Talisman and Abyssal Splinter of Hellfire none of their drawbacks apply.
  - Mighty Throw: Mightiest does not consume this bonus
- Locran's Talisman or Banished Lord's Talisman (equip one and use the other as a Charm), as massive damage multipliers
  - Locran's Talisman, the Critical Strike chance drawback is offset by Ring of Red Furor
  - Banished Lord's Talisman, generates Overpower stacks passively which are consumed by Mighty Throw: Overpower
- Leoric's Crown doubles the effects of Gems and Season 15 Splinters, which makes it a Seasonal best in slot for this build.
- Ramaladni's Magnum Opus, extremely potent Fury-based damage multiplier
  - DO NOT EQUIP THIS ITEM WITHOUT Insight as the added Fury drain is too much to handle
- Stone of Jordan, decent offense and massive defensive boost.
  - NOTE: Make sure that your Physical resistance value is the highest using charms, affixes and tempers
- Insight, as a stat stick with obscene amounts of Fury generation, +%Critical Strike chance and x%Critical Strike Damage
  - Enchant the +Weapon damage into Strength
- Infinity, strong offensive value, added defenses and effortless Vulnerable application to close targets
  - Enchant the +%Gold drop rate into Strength
  - The implicit +%Lucky hit chance to deal a Crushing Blow is very strong to quickly make targets Injured (after which it stops activating)
- Earthstriker's Aspect as your alternative source of Overpower stacks when swapping weapons between Hammer of the Ancients and Mighty Throw every few seconds.
