Uh that was a quick question.  
 Well, in case you ask “how to make a distance joint 3d” :

-Create 2 objects with a rigidbody on each object, then add a configurable joint on one of the object.

-Assign the other object to the **“Connected body”** field

-Set **“X Motion”** to **“Limited”** to limit the distance, in this case you probably want to set it to X , Y and Z

-Under **“Linear Limit”** set **“Limit”** to your wanted distance, you can also edit the spring and damper under **“Linear Limit Spring”**.
