Project: Vibrant Journeys Mod (1.21.4, 1.20.1) – A Whole New Land
Project: Vibrant Journeys Mod aims to add some spice to survival without changing its core aspects. Instead of adding tons of new content in one direction, this mod focuses on adding content that overlays the current style of survival. Here, you will not find complex machinery, or magical spells, or a billion biomes, or too many dimensions to remember. You will find new building materials, new places to explore, and new monsters to slay. It does not aim to revamp or change the focus on survival, but rather to enhance it. (Also comes with full support for Biomes O’ Plenty.)
Features:
- Introduces 14 new biomes, including Aspen Grove, Autumnal Coniferous Forest, Baobab Fields, Blossoming Fields, Boreal Forest, Crystal Lakes, Desert Shrubland, Overgrown Spires, Pine Meadows, Prairie, Redwoods, Snowy Boreal Forest, Snowy Redwoods, and Verdant Sands.
- Adds rocks, twigs, fallen leaves, bones, pinecones, seashells, and ice chunks scattered across different biomes.
- Features tall grass variants, fallen trees, bark mushrooms, and nether flora.
- Lakes now have seagrass, cattails, and lilypads, while beaches include sea oats, seashells, and beach grass.
- Ruined portals now have soul sand, basalt, warped, and crimson variants.
- Introduces Fir, Pine, Redwood, Willow, Palm, Cottonwood, Aspen, Juniper, Baobab, Maple, Cherry Blossom, Tamarack, and Joshua trees, each with unique wood types.
